# High Point Roofing Corp — Homepage Redesign Concept

## Project Type

**Concept redesign / unsolicited homepage concept**

This is a visual and conversion-focused redesign concept for **High Point Roofing Corp**, a Miami roofing company.

The goal is **not** to rebuild the entire website. The goal is to create a polished homepage concept that demonstrates how the company's existing credibility could be presented through a more modern, focused and conversion-oriented experience.

Official website: https://highpointroofingcorp.com/

# 2. Redesign Objective

## Main Goal

Make the website immediately communicate:

> **Who they are + what they do + why they should be trusted + what the visitor should do next.**

The redesign should feel like a premium local roofing company rather than a generic construction website.

## Conversion Goal

Primary conversion:

**Request a Free Roof Analysis**

Secondary conversion:

**Call the Company**

The visitor should never have to search for the next step.

# 20. UX Principles

Follow these rules:

### Rule 1 — The visitor understands the business immediately.

No mystery.

### Rule 2 — Trust comes before complexity.

Show experience, licensing, testimonials and work.

### Rule 3 — One primary CTA.

Free Roof Analysis.

### Rule 4 — Visual hierarchy matters.

The most important content should be visually dominant.

### Rule 5 — Mobile first.

The website must feel excellent on a phone.

### Rule 6 — Reduce cognitive load.

Do not put every piece of information above the fold.

### Rule 7 — Use real proof.

Use real testimonials, real projects and verified business information.

---

# 21. Animation

Use subtle animations only.

Recommended:

- fade-up on section entrance
- image reveal
- subtle card hover
- button hover
- navigation transition
- smooth scrolling

Avoid:

- excessive parallax
- spinning elements
- aggressive motion
- animations that delay content
- unnecessary loading animations

The site should feel premium, not like a demo of animation libraries.

# 26. What NOT to Build

Do not build the entire website.

For the sales pitch, build only:

- Homepage
- Responsive desktop version
- Responsive mobile version
- Main interactions
- CTA/form visual
- A few polished sections

Do not spend days recreating:

- every service page
- blog
- complete CMS
- authentication
- backend
- complex quote system
- complete SEO architecture

The purpose is to demonstrate capability and start a conversation.

# 28. Sales Presentation

When showing the concept, explain the redesign around business outcomes rather than aesthetics.

Do NOT say:

> "I made your website prettier."

Say:

> "I wanted to make your experience, services and trust signals easier for a potential customer to understand, especially on mobile."

Then explain:

### Before

- Information is spread across the page
- Strong trust signals compete with other content
- Main CTA could be more prominent
- Mobile experience can be more focused

### After

- Clear value proposition
- Stronger first impression
- Trust immediately visible
- Services easier to understand
- Stronger CTA hierarchy
- Better mobile conversion path
